Proposed engineering model & limits

Stage A provides provisional static reactions for exactly two vertical supports. Three or more supports remain unresolved. A future beam model would require support and load positions, self-weight distribution, beam stiffness/continuity, rope stiffness and boundary conditions. Equalising mechanisms require their own model. Independent engineering validation is required before operational use.

Stage B uses the ideal vector resultant: R = 2T cos(θ/2). It assumes a static, single rope over one sheave and equal tensions. The result excludes block self-weight, friction-related tension differences, acceleration, shock, side loading and combined mounting loads.
